# Topic backlog

Ordered queue for the daily guide-writer run. Take the first unchecked item, write it, check it off (`- [x]`). When fewer than 7 unchecked items remain, generate 10 more following the "how to add more" rules at the bottom, in the same run, before you stop. The pipeline publishes one guide a day indefinitely, so an empty backlog is a broken pipeline, not a finish line.

## The one rule that outranks everything else here

**A title is a search query, not a headline.** Raz's goal is to be found for what customers actually type into Google, so the title has to read like the query itself, at the front, in the words a customer would use. Not the words the industry uses.

Real queries look like:

- `כמה עולה סרטון UGC בישראל ב-2026` (already live)
- `כמה עולה לבנות אתר לעסק קטן ב-2026` (already live)
- `כמה עולה אתר שבנוי ב-AI`
- `מה עדיף אתר בוורדפרס או אתר ב-AI`
- `למה האתר שלי איטי` (already live)

The patterns that carry the intent, in rough order of commercial value:

| Pattern | Why it converts |
|---|---|
| `כמה עולה …` | Someone pricing a purchase right now |
| `מה עדיף … או …` | Someone at a decision fork, one step from buying |
| `איך לבחור …` | Same fork, earlier |
| `כמה זמן לוקח …` | Scoping a real project |
| `אתר ל<מקצוע>` | Vertical intent, low competition, high close rate |
| `למה … / מה זה …` | Top of funnel, cheap to rank, feeds the cluster |

The keyword goes **first**. `כמה עולה אתר תדמית` then a colon then the qualifier. Never bury the query behind a clever opener: `קמפיין AI מקצה לקצה: איך זה נראה בפועל` is a headline nobody searches for, and it is the exact shape to avoid.

Write the way a customer talks, including slang, even where it doesn't match the service page names. Raz asked explicitly not to rename the service pages, so guides are the only place these queries get covered.

## Priority, and why this order

`keyword-map.md` in this folder has the September 2026 SERP research behind the
ordering. The short version: the head terms (`סרטוני AI לעסקים`, `פרסומות AI`) are
already held by seven Israeli businesses with years of domain history, and the
queue before that research was 27 website topics to 7 AI ones in the market Raz
says is the priority. The three AI sections below come first for that reason.
Do not reorder them back without reading the map.

## AI tool queries · the buyers nobody is answering

`Sora`, `Veo`, `Kling`, `Runway` in Hebrew return Globes and Geektime. A business
owner typing one of these is asking whether it can make their ad, and no supplier
is answering. Cheap to rank, and it is Raz's own daily material. Answer as a
supplier, never as a reviewer: a feature list competes with Geektime and loses.

## Social and UGC reels · high volume, priced at the floor

Researched separately (see `keyword-map.md`). This cluster is contested and, worse,
anchored low: Shotto AI sells a Hebrew UGC video at 50 shekels, שיווקנט sells 12 AI
reels a month at about 200 a reel, IsraModel at 2 shekels an image. Competing on
`כמה עולה רילס AI` lands in a result page where the answer is 50 shekels and a
self-serve tool wins.

So these titles carry the query but answer on the fork the tools cannot cross:
Hebrew that actually lip-syncs, a product that stays itself across every frame, a
concept rather than an avatar reading a script. Never write one of these as a
price-per-reel race.

- [ ] רילס UGC AI לעסק: מה זה באמת ומה ההבדל מאווטאר מדבר

## How to add more

Never add a topic before you have written its title as a query. Say the title out loud as if you were typing it into Google with a credit card in the other hand. If it doesn't sound like something a paying customer types, it isn't a title yet.

Then, before it goes on the list:

1. **Check the `guides` table** (`select slug, title from guides`) so you don't cover a live topic twice under a new slug.
2. **Pick the bucket by intent, not by subject.** Aim for a mix weighted toward the top of the table above: roughly half pricing and comparison, a quarter vertical, a quarter everything else. Pricing and comparison queries are where Raz's money is; "מה זה" articles are filler if they dominate.
3. **Anchor it to something Raz actually sells** (web design, WordPress, AI video and visual content, site upgrades, automations). A guide ranking for work he doesn't do is a wasted day.
4. **Year-tag only where the price or the tooling genuinely moves annually** (pricing guides, platform comparisons). A year tag on an evergreen topic just dates it.
5. **Keep the register** described in `voice-and-structure.md`.
